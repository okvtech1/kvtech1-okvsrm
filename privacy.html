Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Who we are

OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we") sells and supports the School Result and Accounting Management System and operates this website.

What we collect

On this website we collect the details you type into our forms: name, school, email address, phone number, town and state, role, number of pupils, how you heard about us, payment receipt images and payment references, installation requests and messages. If you pay online, Paystack collects your card or bank details; we receive only the payment status, reference and amount.

Why we collect it

To give you the download, answer your questions, confirm payment, issue your activation code, provide installation and support, send updates you asked for, prevent fraud and keep business and tax records.

Where it is kept

Submissions are stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive and sent by Gmail under our Google Workspace account. Alerts may also be forwarded to our own Telegram or other tools. Google and Paystack process data under their own policies.

The school system

The software itself runs on your school computer. We do not receive your pupil, staff or accounting data. Data reaches us only when your administrator submits a form from the License page.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (Google, Paystack, SMS or messaging providers) and when the law requires.

Retention

We keep customer and payment records for as long as needed for support, accounting and legal reasons, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data and may object to marketing. Write to technologyokv@gmail.com.

Children

This website is for school owners and staff, not for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date below. Contact technologyokv@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 810 414 1138.
